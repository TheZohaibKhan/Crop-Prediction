Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers to common questions from our farmers
Is AgriSmart completely free to use?
Yes! All core features — crop prediction, disease detection, fertilizer recommendation, marketplace access, and news — are completely free for registered farmers. We believe every Indian farmer deserves access to modern agricultural tools.
How accurate is the crop prediction model?
Our Random Forest model achieves 95%+ accuracy on validation datasets covering 22 crop types. The model is trained on 2,200+ records from Indian agricultural data. Accuracy is best when you provide precise soil test values.
What crops does disease detection support?
Our VGG19 model supports 14 crops including Apple, Cherry, Corn, Grape, Peach, Pepper, Potato, Strawberry, Tomato, and more — with 38 disease classes in total. More crops are being added each season.
How do I get a soil test done?
You can get your soil tested at any Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), Soil Health Card centre, or accredited private lab. The government's Soil Health Card scheme provides free testing — visit soilhealth.dac.gov.in to find a nearby lab.
Can I use AgriSmart on my mobile phone?
Absolutely! AgriSmart is fully responsive and works on all smartphones, tablets, and computers. No app download is required — just open your mobile browser and visit our website.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
We'd love your feedback! Use the contact form above, email us at help@agrismart.in, or call our helpline. Feature suggestions from farmers directly shape our product roadmap.
