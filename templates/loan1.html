Agricultural Loans & Schemes
Government-backed financial schemes to empower Indian farmers

PM Kisan Credit Card (PMKCC)
Short-term credit for agricultural needs with simplified delivery and interest rate concessions for timely repayment.
Apply Now

Interest Subvention — Crop Loans
Short-term crop loans at subsidized interest. Prompt repayment earns additional interest rate reductions via NABARD.

National Horticulture Mission (NHM)
Crop insurance scheme protecting against natural calamities, pests, and diseases. Heavily subsidized premiums make it affordable.

NMSA supports climate-resilient farming, water management, and resource conservation to build long-term farm sustainability.
