Crop Insurance Schemes
Government-backed insurance schemes to protect farmers from crop losses

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Visit Portal

Comprehensive insurance protection for food crops, oilseeds and annual horticultural/commercial crops notified by state governments. Claims of full Sum Insured are paid without capping or reduction.

2%
of sum insured

Prevented sowing/planting risk: up to 25% of sum insured paid when sowing fails due to adverse weather.

Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (WBCIS)

Visit Portal

Insurance protection for notified food crops, oilseeds and horticultural/commercial crops. Covers weather index deviations including rainfall, temperature, relative humidity, and wind speed.

Coconut Palm Insurance Scheme (CPIS)

Visit Portal

Dedicated insurance protection for Coconut Palm growers. Premium rates vary by plant age group, with generous subsidies to make it accessible for all types of farmers.

Plant age 4–15 years
₹9.00 / palm
Plant age 16–60 years
₹14.00 / palm
Premium subsidy
50–75%
