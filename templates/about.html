Our Mission & Values
What drives us every single day
Farmer-First Technology
Every feature we build starts with one question: does this genuinely help a farmer in Vidarbha, Bundelkhand, or the Punjab plains? We never build for complexity — we build for impact.

Our Journey
How AgriSmart grew from idea to impact
2022 — The Idea
Born in a College Hackathon
A team of engineering students from Pune noticed that farmers nearby had no easy way to predict which crop to sow. AgriSmart started as a weekend project.
2023 — Research & Build
First AI Models Trained
We partnered with two KVKs in Maharashtra to collect real soil data. Our Naive Bayes crop predictor and Random Forest model were trained and validated in the field.
2024 — Launch
Public Platform Goes Live
AgriSmart launched with crop prediction, disease detection using VGG19, fertilizer recommendations, and the farmer marketplace. 500+ farmers signed up in the first month.
2025–2026 — Growth
10,000+ Farmers & Counting
New features — news feed, loan guidance, insurance info, and vendor marketplace — rolled out. Serving farmers across Maharashtra, MP, UP, and Rajasthan.
